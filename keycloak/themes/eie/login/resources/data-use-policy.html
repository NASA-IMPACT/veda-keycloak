Data use policy

  • Use of NASA data that contains Personally Identifying Information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI) is strictly prohibited. No data containing PII or PHI may be uploaded or used within any publicly available capability.
  • Use must not include uploading or inputting any data that NASA has any interest in protecting, which includes any CUI-marked or proprietary content. Data uploaded, used in prompts or queries, or otherwise input into publicly available GenAI services carries an unacceptable risk of unauthorized disclosure.
  • Use of a personal account on government equipment exposes personal account contents to Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests. All content of the account would be included in the FOIA report, including personal content. Likewise, discovery in litigation may expose personal account information. Furthermore, some systems require user agreements that do not align with NASA Policy.
